Professional Bio

Olivia Oya Kaptan is Co-Founder and Creative Director of ObscuronLabs™, leading the company's music, digital media, and content strategy practice. An international performing artist since 1995, she performs in eight languages across jazz, blues, Latin, pop, and soft rock.

Through NeoComus Music, she bridges creative direction with AI-powered content systems — producing music, digital campaigns, and brand media at the intersection of technology and human expression.

Berklee College of Music certified across music business, songwriting, vocal production, and music education. University of Michigan and AMII certified in AI for Creative Work and Generative AI. At ObscuronLabs™, she shapes the creative voice of every client engagement — ensuring that intelligence infrastructure carries a distinctly human aesthetic.
